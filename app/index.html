Road to Platinum

  1. Play the story. Chapters 1–4, the final mission and every story mission — 17 trophies, all unmissable. Just finish the story.
  2. Do the possibly-missable ones when the chance comes. 8 trophies are tied to one-time partner or story moments, flagged ⚠ below. Do them the first time the moment appears.
  3. Finish the side strands. Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus 8 stranger strands across Leonida.
  4. Activities & races. Shooting Range golds, drone races, fishing, arcade games, meditation spots and stunts.
  5. Collectibles & exploration. Card sets, WAiNK spots, vehicles, all 52 areas of Leonida, and every garage.
  6. Money, then 100%. Launder $250,000 in Marked Bills, spend $100,000 in shops, then push to 100% completion — the last gold before the platinum.
